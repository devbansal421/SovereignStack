# Supply-Chain Provenance and Bus Factor Auditing

We evaluate software supply chains by analyzing direct and top-level transitive dependencies for Maintainer Provenance (identity, country, organizational backing) and Bus Factor Risk (single-maintainer abandonment exposure), paired with AI-driven Sovereign Alternative suggestions. This differentiates SovereignStack from conventional CVE scanners (e.g. Snyk/Dependabot) by focusing specifically on maintainer trust, social-engineering takeover vectors (e.g., `xz-utils`, `event-stream`), and national technological sovereignty.

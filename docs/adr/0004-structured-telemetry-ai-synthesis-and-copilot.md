# Structured Telemetry AI Synthesis and Interactive Sovereignty Copilot

We integrate AI at two distinct tiers: (1) deterministic JSON-schema Telemetry Synthesis that maps intercepted network endpoints and manifest data directly to DPDP Act 2023 statutory clauses with plain-English remediations, and (2) an interactive, audit-grounded Sovereignty Copilot chat drawer. This ensures all AI reasoning is strictly tethered to concrete runtime telemetry, avoiding generic legal hallucinations while providing an interactive demonstration surface for evaluators.

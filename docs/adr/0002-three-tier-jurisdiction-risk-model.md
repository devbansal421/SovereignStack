# Three-Tier Jurisdiction Risk Model with Entity Attribution

We adopt a 3-tier geopolitical and legal adequacy model (Sovereign Tier / Adequacy Tier / High-Risk Tier) coupled with a Controlling Entity overlay for auditing Data Flows. Rather than a naive binary "domestic vs foreign" filter or a pure corporate-hegemony score, this model reflects the legal reality of India's DPDP Act 2023 Section 16, distinguishing between compliant cross-border destinations with adequate safeguards and high-risk jurisdictions with extraterritorial state surveillance mandates.
